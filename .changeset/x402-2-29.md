Require `@x402/core` and `@x402/evm` 2.29.0 or later (the SDK's peer range was `^2.27.0`; the CLI pinned 2.27.0). 2.29 carries upstream security fixes, among them a zod floor for GHSA-mrhx-c7xc-m3w5, bounded facilitator response reads and stricter payment-header parsing. With 2.29 an unpaid `HEAD` on a route keyed `GET` gets a `402` instead of running the paid handler, so radius-sdk sellers now charge `HEAD` without a separate `HEAD` route.
